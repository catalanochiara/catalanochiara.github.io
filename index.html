About

I am a licensed psychologist with a research background in psycholinguistics and the neural bases of language, with a particular interest in language processing across the lifespan and in atypical populations.

I am currently a Graduate Research Fellow at IUSS Pavia, working on a project on the neurophysiology of verbal humor in early childhood. I enjoy multidisciplinary research environments and try to combine methodological rigor with genuine curiosity for how language shapes – and is shaped by – the developing mind.
